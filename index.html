Founder Spotlight

Sagar Khadka

Sagar Khadka founded STEP Economics to close the gap between rigorous economic theory and the practical decisions that businesses, policymakers, and students face every day. His approach is shaped simultaneously by a deep academic grounding in economics and eight years spent inside the operating engine of one of Nepal's leading commercial banks—a rare combination that lets him move fluidly between research, strategy, and real-world execution.
